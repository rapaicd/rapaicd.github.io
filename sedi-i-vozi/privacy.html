Ukratko

  • Čuvamo samo ono što upišeš o autu (vozilo, točenja, datume registracije i servisa) i podešavanje valute, da bi ti aplikacija računala potrošnju i troškove i radila na svakom tvom telefonu.

  • Drugi korisnici nikad ne vide tvoje podatke. Poređenje potrošnje koristi samo zbirne vrednosti grupa od najmanje 5 korisnika.
  • Kad aplikacija padne, šalje se tehnički izveštaj o grešci, a broje se i neke radnje (npr. „dodat auto”), preko Google-ovog servisa Firebase. To ne sadrži tvoje unose i nije povezano sa tvojim nalogom, a oba možeš da isključiš u Podešavanjima.
  • Nema reklama i ne prodajemo podatke.
  • Nalog i sve podatke možeš da obrišeš u aplikaciji, odmah i trajno.

Zašto i na osnovu čega

  • Da bi aplikacija radila: čuvanje i sinhronizacija tvojih unosa, računanje potrošnje i troškova, podsetnici, prijava preko Google naloga ako je povežeš. Osnov: izvršenje ugovora, odnosno pružanje usluge koju koristiš (čl. 12 st. 1 tač. 2 Zakona o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti).
  • Zbirna statistika za poređenje: jednom dnevno računamo medijanu potrošnje za grupe istih modela (npr. „Volkswagen Golf, dizel 1,9 l, 2005–2009”). Grupa se prikazuje tek kad ima najmanje 5 različitih korisnika, a svaki korisnik se računa jednom. Pojedinačne vrednosti se nikad ne prikazuju ni ne dele. Osnov: legitimni interes (da aplikacija svakome pokaže kako stoji u odnosu na druge vozače istog modela, bez otkrivanja ičijih pojedinačnih podataka).
  • Bezbednost i otklanjanje kvarova (tehnički podaci). Osnov: legitimni interes.
  • Popravka grešaka (izveštaji o padovima) i poboljšanje aplikacije (statistika korišćenja: koji delovi aplikacije se koriste i da li se grupe za poređenje popunjavaju). Osnov: legitimni interes (da aplikacija radi bez grešaka i da znamo šta treba poboljšati). Oba su podrazumevano uključena i svako posebno možeš da isključiš u Podešavanjima; od tada se ništa ne šalje.

Privacy policy (English)

Effective from: 2 October 2026. If the two versions differ, the Serbian version prevails.

In short

  • We store only what you enter about your car (vehicles, refuels, registration and service dates) and your currency setting, so the app can calculate consumption and costs and work on each of your phones.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number: the account is anonymous. If you choose to link a Google account (so your data survives a change of phone), we store that account's email and name.
  • Other users never see your data. The consumption comparison uses only aggregate values of groups with at least 5 users.
  • When the app crashes, a technical error report is sent, and a few actions (e.g. "car added") are counted, through Google's Firebase service. This contains none of your entries and is not linked to your account; you can turn both off in Settings.
  • No ads, and we do not sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all data yourself in the app, immediately and permanently.

Who is responsible

The controller is Danilo Rapaić, a private individual, Belgrade, Serbia. Contact for privacy questions: danilo.applications@gmail.com.

Why, and on what basis

  • To provide the app: storing and syncing your entries, calculating consumption and costs, reminders, signing in with Google if you link it. Basis: performance of a contract (Art. 12(1)(2) of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection).
  • Aggregate statistics for comparison: once a day we compute the median consumption of groups of the same model. A group is shown only when it has at least 5 different users, and each user counts once. Individual values are never shown or shared. Basis: legitimate interest (showing everyone how they compare with other drivers of the same model, without revealing anyone's individual data).
  • Security and troubleshooting (technical data). Basis: legitimate interest.
  • Fixing bugs (crash reports) and improving the app (usage statistics: which parts of the app are used and whether comparison groups fill up). Basis: legitimate interest (keeping the app free of bugs and knowing what to improve). Both are on by default and you can turn each off in Settings; from then on nothing is sent.

Who else sees the data

No other user. Data is stored by our processor Supabase Inc. (database and sign-in) on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), processing it only on our instructions. The app is distributed through Google Play, which keeps its own records of downloads under its own privacy policy. If you link a Google account, Google performs the sign-in and so learns that you use our app. Crash reports and usage statistics are processed by Google (Firebase) as our processor, possibly on servers in the United States; the transfer relies on the standard contractual clauses included in Google's data processing terms. Google does not use them for advertising: we turned off the advertising ID, Google signals and data sharing with Google. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • As long as your account exists. When you delete the account, all data is deleted immediately and permanently, on the server and on the phone.
  • What you delete in the app (e.g. a car or a refuel) disappears from the app immediately and is deleted from the server for good within 90 days. Until then it is only marked as deleted, so your other phones learn about the deletion too.
  • Anonymous accounts without any data are deleted automatically after 60 days.
  • Crash reports: 90 days. Usage statistics: individual events 2 months, after that only aggregate counts. This data is not linked to the account, so deleting the account does not delete it, but we cannot tell whose it is.

For anything else, write to danilo.applications@gmail.com with the user ID from the "Account" screen; we reply within 30 days. You can lodge a complaint with the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade, www.poverenik.rs.

All communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, every account can read and change only its own data, enforced by the database for every request.
